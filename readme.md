## Project Overview

Doky is a modern healthcare platform designed to simplify booking doctor and hospital appointments, managing patient schedules, and accessing healthcare services from one centralized system.

The platform connects patients with healthcare providers through an easy-to-use, efficient, and organized digital experience.

---

# Problem Statement

Healthcare appointment systems often suffer from several issues that negatively affect both patients and healthcare providers, including:

* Difficulty finding suitable appointment times.
* Complicated or manual booking processes.
* Fragmented healthcare information across multiple systems or platforms.
* Poor schedule organization and coordination.
* Time wasted on phone calls and manual appointment handling.
* Increased risk of scheduling conflicts and human errors.

Doky aims to solve these problems by providing a centralized healthcare booking and management platform.

---

# Target Users

## 1. Patients

Users who want to:

* Search for doctors and hospitals.
* Book appointments easily.
* Manage and track their appointments.
* Access healthcare-related services in one place.

## 2. Healthcare Providers

Doctors, clinics, and hospitals who want to:

* Manage schedules efficiently.
* Receive and organize patient bookings.
* Improve appointment coordination.
* Reduce manual administrative work.

---

# Why People Will Use Doky

Users will use Doky because it:

* Saves time and effort by simplifying appointment booking.
* Provides a single platform for healthcare scheduling and management.
* Offers a clean and easy user experience.
* Improves appointment organization and reduces scheduling mistakes.
* Enhances communication between patients and healthcare providers.
* Makes healthcare access more convenient and modern.

---

# Core Goals

* Simplify healthcare appointment booking.
* Improve schedule management efficiency.
* Centralize healthcare service access.
* Create a modern and user-friendly healthcare experience.
* Reduce operational complexity for healthcare providers.

---

# Platform Vision

Doky aims to become a trusted digital healthcare ecosystem that improves the connection between patients and healthcare providers through technology, simplicity, and accessibility.
